Three leave types for every employee

Balances are calculated automatically and update as soon as a request is approved or cancelled.

Casual leave

For personal errands and short breaks. Limited to 3 per month.

Sick leave

For when you are unwell. Apply at any time, with no notice needed.

Earned leave

For planned time off. Apply at least 7 days in advance.

Leave rules, applied for you

The system checks every request against company policy before it reaches your manager.

  • Maximum 3 casual leaves in a monthRequests beyond the monthly limit are rejected.
  • Sick leave can be applied anytimeNo notice period is required.
  • Earned leave needs 7 days' noticeShort-notice earned leave is not accepted.
  • Half-day leave is supportedA half day deducts only half a day from your balance.
  • Weekends are not countedSaturdays and Sundays do not use up your leave.
  • Public holidays are not countedHolidays in your range are skipped automatically.

No more empty departments

If employees in the same department are already on leave and 30% of the department is away, the next request is rejected automatically. Your team stays covered without anyone having to check a calendar.

Example: in a department of 10, three people on leave reaches the 30% limit, so a fourth request for the same dates is rejected.

How a leave request works

From application to decision, the checking is done before a manager ever sees the request.

Apply

Choose the leave type, the dates, and full or half day.

Rules and conflicts checked

Notice period, monthly limits, weekends, holidays and department cover are checked instantly.

Manager decides

Your manager approves or rejects the request and adds a comment.

Balance updates

Your remaining leave is recalculated and the request appears in your history.

Reports that answer real questions

HR can see who is away, where, and how much leave is left.

  • Monthly leave report for all leave taken in a month
  • Department-wise report to compare teams
  • Most absent employee to spot patterns early
  • Remaining leave balance for every employee

Secure sign-in
